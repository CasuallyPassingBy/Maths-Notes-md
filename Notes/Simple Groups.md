**Prop:** Let $G$ be a finite group of order $pq$, with $p >q$ primes, then $G$ is not simple.

**Prop:** Let $G$ be a finite group of order $p^2q$, with $p,q$ primes, then $G$ is not simple.

$(*)$**Burnside Theorem:** Let $G$ be a finite group of order $p^rq^s$, with $p >q$ primes and $r+s> 1$, then $G$ is not simple.

**Th:** $A_n$ is simple for every $n \ge 5$. 

**Th:** Let $G$ be a finite simple abelian group, then $G$ is a cyclic group of prime order.
