**Th:** Let $S \subseteq \Bbb R^n$ is a nonempty, closed convex set and $y \notin S$. There there exists a unique point $x_0 \in S$ with minimum distance from $y$. Furthermore, $x_0$ is the minimising point iff $(y - x_0)^\top (x - x_0) \le 0$ for all $x \in S$.
