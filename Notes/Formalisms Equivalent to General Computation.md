Subjects: [[Theory of Computation]]

Each one of these formalisms embodies a notion of *computation* in one form or another, and each can simulate the others. All of the following are equivalent:
- [[Turing Machines]],
- [[Post Systems|Post systems]],
- [[Type 0 Grammars]],
- [[Gödel Recursive Functions]],
- [[Lambda Calculus]],
- [[Combinatory Logic]],
- [[While Programs]]
