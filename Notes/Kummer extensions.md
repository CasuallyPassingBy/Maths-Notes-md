The theorems that talk about how a cyclic extension is actually a simple radical extension on fields with the appropriate roots of unity can be generalised. 

Let $L$ be the splitting fields of polynomials of the form$$(x^n -a_1)\cdots (x^n-a_k)$$where $a_i \in K$, and $\text{char }K \not\mid n$, and $K$ has the $n$th roots of unity. Then $L/K$ is called a *Kummer extension of exponent $n$*. 

**Prop:** Let $L/K$ be Jummer extension of exponent $n$, then $\text{Gal}(L/K)$ is an abelian group that has exponent that divides $n$. 

**Th:** Let $L/K$ be an abelian extension with Galois group of exponent $n$, and suppose that $\text{char }K\not\mid n$, and $K$ has the $n$th roots of unity, then $L/K$ is a Kummer extension of degree $n$.
