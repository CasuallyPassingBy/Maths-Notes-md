**Lemma:** Every covering space of a graph is also a graph with vertices the lifts of the vertices and edges in the base graph. 

**Nielsen-Schreier Theorem:** Every subgroup of a free group is free

**Nielsen-Schreier Formula:** If $G$ is a free group of rank $n$, and $H$ is a subgroup of finite index $[G: H] = e$, then $H$ is free of rank $1+e(n-1)$.
