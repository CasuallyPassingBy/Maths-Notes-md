Let $X$ be a topological space with topology $\tau$. A *hyperspace of* $X$ is a specified collection of subsets of $X$ with a topology related to $\tau$.

Some examples of hyperspaces are:
- [[Vietoris Hyperspace]]
- [[Compact Vietoris Hyperspace]]
- [[Finite Vietoris Hyperspace]]
- [[Pixley-Roy Hyperspace]]
