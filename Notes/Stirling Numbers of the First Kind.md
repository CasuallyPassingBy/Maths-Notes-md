There are two kinds of Stirling numbers of the first kind: signed and unsigned. This makes it a little bit annoying. 

The Stirling numbers of the first kind are the coefficients $s(n, k)$ in the expansion of the falling factorial: $$x^{\underline n} = \sum_{k = 0}^n s(n, k) x^k.$$The unsigned Stirling numbers of the first kinda are the coefficients $\left[{n \atop k}\right]$ or $c(n, k)$  in the expansion of the rising factorial $$x^\overline n = \sum_{k = 0}^n \left[{n \atop k}\right] x^k.$$
