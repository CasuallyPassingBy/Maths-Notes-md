**Def:** Let $V$ be a real vector space of dimension $n > 1$, and let $K = (k_1,\dots, k_m)$ be a finite sequence of integers satisfying $0 < k_1 < \cdots < k_m < n$. A *flag in $V$ of type $K$* is a nested sequence of linear subspaces $S_1\subseteq S_2\subseteq \cdots \subseteq S_m \subseteq V$, with $\dim S_i = k_i$ for each $i$. The set of all flags of type $K$ in $V$ is denoted by $\text F_K(V)$. We see that $\text{GL}(V)$ acts transitively $\text F_K(V)$ with a closed subgroup as isotropy group, so $\text F_K(V)$ has a unique smooth manifold structure making it into a homogeneous $\text{GL}(V)$-space. With this structure, $\text F_K(V)$ is called a *flag manifold.*

**Prop:** Let $\text F_K(V)$ be the set of flags of type $K$ in a finte-dimensional vector space $V$. Then $\text{GL}(V)$ acts transitively on $\text F_K(V)$, and the isotropy group of a particular flag is a closed subgroup of $\text{GL}(V)$, and that $\text F_K(V)$ has a unique smooth manifold structure such that the action is smooth.
