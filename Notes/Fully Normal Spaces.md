**Def:** A topological space $X$ is *fully normal* if for every open cover of $X$ has an open star-refinement. Additionally, we say that $X$ is *fully $T_4$* if it is fully normal and $T_1$. 

**Obs:** Every fully normal space is paracompact.

**Obs:** Every open cover in a fully normal space is normal.

**Prop:** A topological space $X$ is fully normal iff every open cover of $X$ has an open barycentric refinement.

**Prop:** Every fully normal space is normal.

We have that the property of being fully normal is behaving like both compact-like property and a separation property. This is really interesting, because it is a stronger version of paracompactness but has to separate closed sets.

**Remark:** The product of fully normal spaces is not necessarily to be normal.

**Prop:** The following statements are true.
- If every open cover of $X$ has a closed locally finite refinement, then $X$ is fully normal.
- If every locally finite open cover of $X$ has a closed locally finite refinement, then every locally finite open cover has a locally finite open barycentric refinement. 

**Prop:** If $X$ is fully normal, then every open cover of $X$ has an open $\sigma$-discrete refinement.

**Prop:** Every $T_2$ paracompact space is fully normal. 

**Cor:** Every $T_2$ compact space is fully $T_4$.

**Cor:** Every $T_3$ Lindelöf space is fully $T_4$.

**Prop:** Every fully normal space is collectionwise normal.

**Stone's coincidence theorem:** A $T_2$ space $X$ is paracompact iff it is fully normal.
