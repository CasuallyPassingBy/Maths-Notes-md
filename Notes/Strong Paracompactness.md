**Def:** A topological space $X$ is said to be *strongly paracompact* or to have the *star-finite property* if every open cover of $X$ has a star-finite open refinement.

**Obs:** Every compact space is strongly paracompact. Every strongly paracompact space is paracompact.

**Prop:** Every regular Lindelöf space is strongly paracompact.

**Prop:** Let $X$ be a connected regular space. $X$ is strongly paracompact iff it is Lindelöf.

**Obs:** Strong paracompactness is hereditary with respect to closed sets.

**Remark:** Neither the product of two strongly paracompact spaces nor the sum of two paracompact closed sets be strongly paracompact.

**Prop:** Every strongly paracompact space is fully normal.
