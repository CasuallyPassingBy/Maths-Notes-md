**Th:** Let $F:M \to \Bbb R^m$ be any smooth map, where $M$ is a smooth $n$-manifold and $m \ge 2n$. For any $\varepsilon > 0$ there is a smooth immersion $\tilde F: M \to \Bbb R^m$ such that $\sup_M\|\tilde F - F\| \le \varepsilon$.

**Whitney Immersion Theorem:** Every smooth $n$-manifold admits an immersion into $\Bbb R^{2n}$.

**Th:** Let $M$ be a smooth $n$-manifold, and suppose $m \ge 2n+1$ and $F: M \to \Bbb R^m$ is an immersion. Then for any $\varepsilon > 0$ there is an injective immersion $\tilde F: M \to \Bbb R^m$ such that $\sup_M\|\tilde F - F\| \le \varepsilon$.

**Lemma:** Suppose $M\subseteq \Bbb R^N$ is a smooth $n$-dimensional submanifold with or without boundary. For any $v\in \Bbb R^N \setminus \Bbb R^{N-1}$, let $\pi_v: \Bbb R^N \to \Bbb R^{N-1}$ be the projection with kernel $\Bbb Rv$ (where we identify $\Bbb R^{N-1}$ with the subspace of $\Bbb R^N$ consisting of points with last coordinate $0$). If $N > 2n+1$, then there is a dense subset of vectors $v\in\Bbb R^N \setminus \Bbb R^{N-1}$ for which $\pi_v|_M$ is an injective immersion of $M$ into $\Bbb R^{N-1}$. 

**Lemma:** Let $M$ be a smooth $n$-manifold with or without boundary. If $M$ admits a smooth embedding into $\Bbb R^N$ for some $N$, then it admits a proper smooth embedding into $\Bbb R^{2n+1}$. 

**Whitney Embedding Theorem:** Every smooth $n$-manifold admits a proper smooth embedding into $\Bbb R^{2n+1}$.

**Cor:** Every smooth $n$-manifold with or without boundary is diffeomorphic to a properly  embedded submanifold $\Bbb R^{2n+1}$ 

**Cor:** Every smooth manifold is metrizable.

**Cor:** Suppose $M$ is a compact smooth $n$-manifold with or without boundary. If $N \ge 2n+1$, then every smooth map from $M$ to $\Bbb R^N$ can be uniformly approximated by embeddings.

$(*)$ **Strong Whitney Immersion Theorem:** Every smooth $n$-manifold admits an immersion into $\Bbb R^{2n-1}$.

$(*)$ **Strong Whitney Embedding Theorem:** Every smooth $n$-manifold admits a proper smooth embedding into $\Bbb R^{2n}$.
