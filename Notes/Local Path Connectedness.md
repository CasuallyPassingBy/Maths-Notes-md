**Def:** We say that a space $X$ is *locally path-connected* if it admits a a basis of path-connected open subsets. This means that for any $p\in X$ and any neighbourhood $U$ of $p$, there is a path-connected neighbourhood $V$ of $p$ such that $p\in V \subseteq U$

**Prop:** Every manifold (with or without boundary) is locally connected and a locally path-connected. 

**Prop:** Suppose $X$ is locally path-connected space.
- $X$ is locally connected 
- Every open subset of $X$ is locally path-connected.
- Every path component of $X$ is open.
- The path components of $X$ are equal to its components.
- $X$ is connected iff it is path-connected.

This means that local path-connectedness is hereditary with respect to open sets. 

**Cor:** A topological manifold is connected iff it is path-connected
