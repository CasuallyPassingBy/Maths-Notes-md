Let $K$ be a field with characteristic $0$. 

**Def:** Let us consider a tensor that is either contravariant or covariant of the form $X_1\otimes X_2 \otimes \dots \otimes X_p$, where $X$ denotes respectively either a vector or a covector, and the indices enumerate such elements. The operator $\text{Alt}$ is called *alternator* if defined in the following way: $$\text{Alt}(X_1\otimes X_2 \otimes \dots \otimes X_p) := \frac{1}{p!} \sum_{\sigma \in S_p} \text{sgn}(\sigma) X_{\sigma(1})\otimes X_{\sigma(2)} \otimes \dots \otimes X_{\sigma(p)}.$$For the other cases, this definition is generalised by linearity. The operator $\text{Alt}$ is a projection operator
