**Def:** Let $X$ be a topological space.
- A *filter $\mathcal F$ (on $X$) converges to a point* $x\in X$, and $x$ is a *limit point* of $\mathcal F$, if every neighbourhood of $x$ belong to the filter $\mathcal F$. To denote this fact it is written as $x \in \lim \mathcal F$.
- We say that a *prefilter $\mathcal B$ converge*s to a point $x \in X$, and $x$ is a *limit point of $\mathcal B$*, if the filter generated by it converges to $x$, this is denoted as $x \in \lim \mathcal {F_B}$.

**Def:** Let $X$ be a topological space:
- We say that a point $x\in X$ is a *cluster point of the filter* $\mathcal F$ if $x\in \bigcap\{\text{cl}(F) \mid F \in \mathcal F\}$. 
- We say that a point $x\in X$ is a *cluster point of the prefilter $\mathcal B$* if it is of the  generated filter $\mathcal {F_B}$ by $\mathcal B$.
This implies that every cluster point of an ultra filter is a limit point of an ultra filter.

We say that the filter $\mathcal F'$ is *finer* than the filter $\mathcal F$ if $\cal F' \supseteq F$. 

**Prop:** If $x$ is a cluster point of the filter $\cal F'$ that is finer than the the filer $\cal F$, then $x$ is cluster point of $\cal F$. If $x$ is a limit point of the filter $\cal F$, then $x$ is also a limit point of every filter $\cal F'$ finer than $\cal F$. If $x$ is cluster point of the filter $\cal F$, then $x$ is a limit of a filter $\cal F'$ that is finer than $\cal F$. 

**Example:** Given a sequence $s = (x_n)_{n <\omega}$ in a topological space $X$, we can consider the set $S_{m} = \{x_n\mid n \ge m\}$ for each $m < \omega$. The collection $\mathcal S= \{S_m \mid m < \omega\}$ is a prefilter. The filter generated by $\mathcal S$, $\mathcal {F_S}$ is called the *generated filter by the sequence $(x_n)_{n <\omega}$*.

**Prop:** Let $s = (x_n)_{n <\omega}$ a sequence on the topological space $X$. The sequence $s$ converges to a point $p\in X$ (respectively, $p$ is a cluster point of $s$) iff the filter generated by $s$, $\mathcal {F_S}$ converges to $p$ (respectively, $p$ is cluster point of $\mathcal {F_S}$).

**Th:** Let $A$ be subset of a topological space $X$. For any $x\in X$, $x \in \text{cl}_X(A)$ iff there's a prefilter $\mathcal B$ formed by subsets of $A$ such that $x \in \lim \mathcal B$ on $X$.

**Th:** Let $X$ and $Y$ be topological spaces, $f: X \to Y$ a function and $x\in X$. The function $f$ is continuous on $x$ iff for every filter $\mathcal G$ on $X$ that converges to $x$, we have that the prefilter $f[\mathcal G]$ converges to $f(x)$ on $Y$.

**Prop:** Let $X$ and $Y$ be topological space, and $f: X \to Y$ be a function. $f$ is continuous if for every prefilter $\mathcal B$ in the space $X$ and the prefilter $f[\mathcal B]$ in the space $Y$ we have $$f[\lim \mathcal B] \subseteq \lim f[\mathcal B].$$
**Prop:** A topological space $X$ is a $T_2$ space iff every filter on $X$ has at most one limit. 

**Th:** For every net $(x_\lambda)_{\lambda\in \Lambda}$ in a topological space, the family $\mathcal F((x_\lambda)_{\lambda\in \Lambda})$ consisting of all sets $A \subseteq X$ with the property that there exists a $\lambda_0\in \Lambda$ such that $x_\lambda \in A$ whenever $\lambda \ge \lambda_0$ is f filter in the subspace and $\lim\mathcal F((x_\lambda)_{\lambda\in \Lambda}) = \lim_{\lambda\in \Lambda_0} x_\lambda$. If the net $(x_\sigma)_{\sigma\in \Sigma}$ is finer than the net $(x_\lambda)_{\lambda\in \Lambda}$, then the filter $\mathcal F ((x_\sigma)_{\sigma\in \Sigma})$ is finer than the filter $\mathcal F((x_\lambda)_{\lambda\in \Lambda})$. 

**Th:** Let $\mathcal F$ be a filter in a topological space $X$; let us denote $\Lambda$ the set of all pairs $(x, A)$, where $x\in A \in \cal F$ and let us define $(x_1, A_1) \le (x_2, A_2)$ if $A_2 \subseteq A_1$. The set $\Lambda$ is directed by $\le$, and for the net $(x_\lambda)_{\lambda\in \Lambda}$ is defined as $x_\lambda = x$ for $\lambda = (x, A) \in \Lambda$, we have that $\mathcal F = \mathcal F (\Lambda(\mathcal F))$ and $\lim_{\lambda \in \Lambda} x_\lambda = \lim \cal F$. 

**Th:** A topological space $X$ is compact iff every [[Convergence of Filters|filter]] in $X$ has a cluster point.
