**Def:** We say that a space $X$ is *locally connected* if it admits a a basis of connected open subsets. This means that for any $p\in X$ and any neighbourhood $U$ of $p$, there is a connected neighbourhood $V$ of $p$ such that $p\in V \subseteq U$

**Prop:** Every manifold (with or without boundary) is locally connected and a locally path-connected. 

**Prop:** Suppose $X$ is a locally connected space.
- Every open subset of $X$ is locally connected.
- Every component of $X$ is open.

This means that local connectedness is hereditary with respect to open sets.
